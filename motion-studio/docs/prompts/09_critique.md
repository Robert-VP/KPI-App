# Opus seine eigenen Frames ansehen lassen

Bilder erzeugen: `bash scripts/review.sh out/final.mp4 4.2`

```
Open out/contact.png, out/strip.png and out/phone.png and look at them properly.
Be a harsh motion director, not a proud author.

Score 1-10: hook in first 2s, readability at phone size, motion quality, variety
(new thing every 2-4s), composition, brand accuracy, sound sync.

List the 3 biggest problems with timestamps.
Hunt for: text overlapping during swaps, anything sliding instead of easing, corner labels
and frame borders, centered-on-gradient shots, blurry scaled text, a dead beat with nothing
happening, a stutter at the loop seam.

Fix them, re-render only the affected seconds, show me the new contact sheet and new scores.
```
